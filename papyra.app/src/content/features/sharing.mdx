## Links that expire

Create a public link to a note and choose how it ends: on a date, after a set
number of views, or never. Recipients need no account. A link can grant read
access or editing.

Links that have expired or run out of views are cleaned up by a background job
rather than lingering as dead rows, and you can revoke any of them by hand at
any moment.

## Sharing with someone on your server

Share directly with another account and the note appears in their **Shared with
me**. Their access is exactly what you granted — view or edit — and it ends when
you revoke it.

Whatever route you use, the note's card in your grid carries a badge saying it
has left the vault, with the names on hover. Papyra's whole pitch is that your
notes are yours, which makes "who else can read this one" worth showing on the
card rather than burying it two clicks away.

## Wiki links and backlinks

Type `[[` in the editor to link another note; a typeahead completes it by title.
Every note then shows its **Linked mentions** underneath — the notes pointing at
this one, each with the sentence that does the pointing.

That is what makes a pile of notes into something you can walk. You follow links
forward while writing and backwards while remembering.

## Mentioning a person shares a paragraph, not a note

Type `@` and a username. The paragraph you named them in is delivered to that
person's Inbox — that block, not the note it lives in.

Papyra then asks whether you also want to share the whole note. Say no and the
paragraph is genuinely all they can see. Say yes and they can open the note
until you revoke it from the note's share menu. The default is the narrow one.

Two rules keep this from leaking:

- A **locked note is never shared and never delivers a mention**, no matter what
  you type in it.
- If the source note is later deleted or locked, the inbox entry says so plainly
  rather than quietly showing stale text — the grant is block-scoped, so there
  is nothing else to fall back to.

## Smart collections

A collection is a saved rule rather than a folder: any note tagged `work` **and**
pinned, or anything whose text mentions pricing. Build the rule in the UI, and
the collection stays current as you write. Notes are not moved or copied — a
note can belong to as many collections as match it, and to none.
